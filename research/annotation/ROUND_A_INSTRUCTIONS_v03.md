# SEVEN — ROUND A INSTRUCTIONS v03

Status: REVIEW CANDIDATE / NOT FROZEN
Protocol: ../SEVEN_ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL_v03.md

## What this round is

You will read short hypothetical behavioural cases and describe, in your own words, what is going on. You are not given any list of categories, labels, or definitions in this round, and you should not look for one.

This round is deliberately free. Your own vocabulary is the data.

## For each case

Fill in two fields on your sheet:

1. **FREE_MOTIVE_DESCRIPTION** — one or two sentences answering: *What motive, tension, or process seems most important in this case?*
2. **KEYWORDS** — up to three short words or phrases naming the motive(s) you see, separated by semicolons (for example: `fear of loss; wanting to be liked`). Use any words you like.

Write what the text supports. If a case seems ordinary, or if you think the main mechanism is something unusual, say so plainly. If you cannot tell, say that.

## Rules

- Work in the order given. Do not skip cases or go back and edit earlier ones after seeing later ones.
- Do not discuss the cases with anyone until all ratings are locked.
- Do not search for or read any SEVEN material, repository, or commentary before your ratings are locked.
- Do not use outside tools to write your descriptions.
- You may stop and resume. Note the total time you spent on the disclosure form.

## When you are done

Return your completed sheet. You will receive Round B only after your Round A sheet has been received. Round A answers cannot be changed after you receive Round B.
