## 6. Central thematic/research question for Book I

The title `Жадность / GREED` is NOT primarily a moral label for the protagonist.

Author direction:
- examine greed/desire/fear of loss as a human vulnerability across the ecosystem;
- especially investigate why victims with meaningful savings or resources can still be drawn into scams promising more, faster returns, recovery of losses, prizes, arbitrage or protection from loss;
- do not flatten victims into fools;
- do not reduce every fraud victim to greed alone;
- show how greed can appear as hope, opportunity, fear of losing what one already has, refusal to accept a loss, desire to improve one's life, easy income or the wish for "just a little more";
- protagonist, account holders, organizers, operators and victims may each express different versions of the same vulnerability.

Research is intended to cover real scam ecosystems, victim psychology, manipulation mechanisms, red flags, investigative consequences and social context.

Safety/content boundary:
The novel/research must NOT become an operational manual for laundering stolen money, building mule networks or running scams.
