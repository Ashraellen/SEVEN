## Book I

Working title:
`GREED / ЖАДНОСТЬ`

Retained author-directed core:
- full-length adult psychological crime novel;
- intended minimum scale approximately 75–80k words;
- protagonist is an immigrant from an unspecified authoritarian country living in an unnamed Eastern European country;
- ordinary precarious work precedes involvement in a scheme presented as corporate tax avoidance/reduction;
- he sources people/accounts while initially believing the illegality concerns tax avoidance rather than theft from private victims;
- later discovery reveals fraud proceeds flowing through the accounts;
- attempted exit becomes dangerous;
- present-time investigator/interrogation frame may open into cinematic flashbacks;
- story must not glamorize criminal participation or become an operational fraud manual.

The title `GREED` is not primarily a moral label for the protagonist. The novel examines desire for more, fear of loss, refusal to accept loss, hope, opportunity, precariousness and exploitation across victims, intermediaries and organizers.
